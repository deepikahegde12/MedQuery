# Headache and migraine

## Migraine overview

Migraine is a recurring type of headache that can cause moderate to severe throbbing or pulsing pain, often on one side, and may occur with nausea, weakness, or sensitivity to light and sound. Triggers vary among people and can include stress, changes in sleep, some sensory exposures, skipped meals, or medication overuse. Not every headache is migraine.

## Assessment

A health professional considers symptom history and examination and may assess for other causes. MedlinePlus advises prompt medical help for sudden severe headaches, headache after a head injury, or headache with stiff neck, fever, confusion, loss of consciousness, or eye or ear pain. New speech, vision, movement, or balance problems also call for urgent assessment. For immediate danger, use local emergency services.
