# Anxiety: general information

## What anxiety can feel like

Anxiety is a feeling of fear, dread, or uneasiness. It can be a normal response to stress. It may cause restlessness, tension, sweating, or a rapid heartbeat. Anxiety disorders involve anxiety that does not go away or worsens and interferes with ordinary activities, relationships, school, or work. Symptoms alone do not establish a diagnosis.

## Getting support

If anxiety is persistent, hard to manage, or affecting daily life, a health care provider can discuss symptoms and whether an assessment or mental health referral may help. Anxiety symptoms can overlap with other health conditions, so assessment matters. Psychotherapy and medicines are among the treatments described by MedlinePlus; choices require individualized discussion with a qualified professional.

## Urgent help

If a person may harm themselves or someone else, or cannot remain safe, seek immediate help through local emergency services or an appropriate local crisis service.
