# Stress and wellbeing

## What stress means

Stress is a physical or emotional response to a challenge or demand. Short-lived stress is common; stress that persists can affect sleep, concentration, mood, and physical wellbeing. People respond differently, and these effects do not by themselves identify a particular disorder.

## General ways people manage stress

MedlinePlus describes approaches that may support wellbeing, including regular physical activity, relaxing activities such as breathing or meditation, enough sleep, limiting excess caffeine, prioritizing what needs attention, and asking trusted people for support. These are general options, not a treatment plan, and they may not be appropriate for everyone.

## When to ask for help

Consider speaking with a health professional when stress feels overwhelming, affects health, disrupts usual activities, or does not improve. Seek immediate help if there are thoughts of suicide, inability to cope safely, or increased substance use that creates immediate danger. Use local emergency or crisis services.
