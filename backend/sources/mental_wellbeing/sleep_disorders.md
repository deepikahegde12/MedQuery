# Summary

## What is sleep?

Sleep is a natural process that helps restore energy and supports learning and memory. Sleep quality matters as well as the number of hours. Poor-quality sleep can affect physical and mental health, thinking, and daily functioning.

## What are sleep disorders?

Sleep disorders disturb normal sleep patterns. Examples include insomnia (trouble falling or staying asleep), sleep-related breathing disorders such as sleep apnea, movement disorders such as restless legs syndrome, hypersomnia (extreme daytime sleepiness), circadian rhythm disorders, and parasomnias such as sleepwalking.

Daytime tiredness can also result from not getting enough sleep, and sleep needs vary with age, lifestyle, health, and sleep patterns. MedlinePlus says most adults need about 7–9 hours each night. A health care provider can assess ongoing symptoms; sleep disorders may be evaluated using medical and sleep history, an exam, or a sleep study.
