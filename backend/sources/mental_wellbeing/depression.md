# Depression: general information

## Possible signs

Depression is more than a brief change in mood. Signs can include ongoing sadness or emptiness, loss of interest in usual activities, changes in sleep or appetite, low energy, hopelessness, difficulty concentrating, unexplained aches, withdrawing from others, or thoughts of death or suicide. Experiences vary, and a list of symptoms cannot diagnose a person.

## When to seek support

Someone who is concerned about persistent mood changes or difficulty managing daily activities can speak with a health care provider or mental health professional. An assessment considers symptoms, health history, and other possible explanations. Effective treatments are available, but the appropriate approach is individual.

## Urgent help

Thoughts of suicide, risk of self-harm, or inability to stay safe call for immediate support. Contact the local emergency service or crisis support available where the person is. If safe, involve a trusted person and do not leave someone at imminent risk alone.
