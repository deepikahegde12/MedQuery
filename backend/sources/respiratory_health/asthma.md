# Asthma

## What is asthma?

Asthma is a long-term lung condition in which airways can become inflamed and narrowed. This can cause wheezing, coughing, chest tightness, or difficulty breathing. A worsening episode is often called an asthma attack or flare-up. Triggers differ and can include allergens, smoke, infections, activity, or other exposures.

## Assessment and care

A health professional assesses symptoms and medical history and may use breathing tests. People with asthma should discuss an individualized plan with their clinician, including what to do if symptoms worsen. Severe difficulty breathing or a rapidly worsening attack needs urgent emergency assistance through local services.
