# Pneumonia

## What is pneumonia?

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames air sacs in one or both lungs. It can be caused by different germs, and severity ranges from mild to serious. Possible symptoms include fever, chills, cough (sometimes with phlegm), shortness of breath, and chest pain with breathing or coughing. Older adults or people with serious illness may have less typical symptoms, including new confusion or weakness.

## When to seek care

Breathing difficulty, blue or grey lips or skin, new confusion, or rapidly worsening illness needs urgent professional assessment. Use local emergency services if there may be immediate danger. A clinician determines the cause and appropriate care.
