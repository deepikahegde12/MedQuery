# Summary

## What is blood pressure?

Blood pressure is the force of blood pushing against artery walls. Systolic pressure is measured when the heart beats; diastolic pressure is measured while the heart rests between beats. A reading has both numbers, such as 120/80.

## How is high blood pressure diagnosed?

High blood pressure usually has no symptoms. A health care provider checks blood pressure using a cuff and takes two or more readings at separate appointments before making a diagnosis. For children and teens, the provider compares readings with those typical for children of the same age, height, and sex.

When blood pressure stays high over time, the heart has to work harder. This can lead to health problems including heart attack, stroke, heart failure, and kidney failure.
