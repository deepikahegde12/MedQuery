# Preventive health checks and screening

## What screening means

Screening tests look for selected conditions before symptoms appear. Examples include checks for high blood pressure, diabetes, and high cholesterol. A screening result is not always a diagnosis; follow-up may be needed to interpret an abnormal result.

## Choosing checks

Whether and when to screen depends on factors such as age, personal and family history, risk factors, prior results, and local clinical guidance. Blood pressure is measured with a cuff. Diabetes screening may use blood glucose tests such as A1C. Cholesterol testing measures lipids in blood. Ask a qualified health professional which checks apply and how often; schedules can differ across countries and individuals.
