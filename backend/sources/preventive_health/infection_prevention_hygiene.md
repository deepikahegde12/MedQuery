# Infection prevention and hand hygiene

## Hand hygiene

Cleaning hands at appropriate times helps reduce the spread of infectious diseases. WHO and UNICEF community guidance recommends washing with plain soap and water, rubbing all hand surfaces, and making clean water and soap or alcohol-based hand rub accessible. Important times include before preparing or eating food, after using the toilet or handling faeces, after coughing or sneezing, and when hands are visibly dirty.

## Other prevention measures

Follow public-health and workplace guidance relevant to the infection and location. Keep food and water safe, stay up to date with locally recommended vaccines, and follow instructions for respiratory or other contagious illness. Needs vary by pathogen and setting; hand hygiene does not replace other prevention measures.
