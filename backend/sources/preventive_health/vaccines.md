# Summary

## What are vaccines?

Vaccines teach the immune system to recognize and defend against harmful germs. Vaccines can protect against diseases caused by viruses, including flu and COVID-19, and bacteria, including tetanus, diphtheria, and pertussis.

## How does immunity work?

The immune system helps fight a germ and can remember it, allowing the body to respond if it invades again. Vaccines can also help protect people around the person vaccinated by making it harder for some germs to spread through a community.

## Safety and schedules

MedlinePlus says vaccines go through safety testing and evaluation before approval in the United States. Recommended vaccines, groups, and timing depend on the applicable health authority and individual circumstances. This general summary is not a personalized vaccine schedule; ask a qualified local health professional about personal recommendations.
