# Summary

## What is an A1C test?

A1C is a blood test for type 2 diabetes and prediabetes. It measures average blood glucose over the past three months. Health professionals may use it alone or with other diabetes tests to help make a diagnosis, and may also use it to monitor diabetes. It differs from the blood sugar checks some people with diabetes do every day.

The result is given as a percentage. MedlinePlus describes values below 5.7% as normal, 5.7% to 6.4% as prediabetes, and above 6.5% as type 2 diabetes. These ranges are general information; a clinician interprets results with the person's circumstances and other tests. An A1C goal for someone who has diabetes can differ from the goal for another person; ask the treating clinician about an individual result.
