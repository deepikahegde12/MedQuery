# Thyroid disorders

## Thyroid overview

The thyroid is a gland in the neck that makes hormones involved in how the body uses energy. Thyroid disorders can cause the gland to make too much or too little hormone, or can involve enlargement or nodules. Symptoms vary and may overlap with other conditions; fatigue or weight change alone does not establish a thyroid disorder.

## Assessment

A health professional considers symptoms and medical history and may order blood tests or other evaluation. Testing and care depend on the suspected problem and individual circumstances. Discuss persistent or concerning symptoms with a clinician rather than self-diagnosing from a symptom list.
