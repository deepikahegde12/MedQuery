# Summary

## What is GERD?

Gastroesophageal reflux (GER) can happen when stomach contents move back up and irritate the esophagus. Occasional reflux is common. Symptoms two or more times a week, or reflux that damages the esophagus lining, may indicate gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), a chronic condition.

## What are common symptoms?

Heartburn is the most common symptom, but GERD can occur without heartburn. Other symptoms may include tasting acid or food in the back of the mouth, a dry cough or hoarse voice, asthma-like symptoms, or trouble swallowing.

## When is urgent medical help needed?

MedlinePlus advises getting medical help right away for chest pain with shortness of breath, or pain in the jaw or arm, because these can be signs of a heart problem. Do not assume chest pain is reflux.
