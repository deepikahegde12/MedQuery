# Diarrhea and constipation

## Diarrhea

Diarrhea means loose, watery stools; it is often short-lived but can have many causes, including infections, medicines, and digestive conditions. Fluid loss can cause dehydration, which may be especially serious in infants, older adults, and people with weakened immune systems. Seek professional advice for signs of dehydration, blood or pus in stool, severe pain, high fever, or persistent symptoms. Children and infants may need earlier assessment.

## Constipation

Constipation generally means bowel movements are less frequent than usual or stools are hard and difficult to pass. Causes vary and may include diet, activity, medicines, or health conditions. Persistent or recurrent constipation, severe abdominal pain, vomiting, blood in stool, or inability to pass stool or gas warrants medical advice.
