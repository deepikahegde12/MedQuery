# Common skin conditions

## Examples

Many conditions can affect skin. Common examples include acne, eczema (which can cause dry, itchy, inflamed skin), hives, psoriasis (which can cause itchy scaly patches), skin infections, and rashes. Similar-looking changes may have different causes, and appearance alone may not identify the condition.

## General skin care and assessment

MedlinePlus suggests gentle skin care, washing cuts with soap and water, protecting skin from injury and sun exposure, and using moisturizers for dry skin. A new, spreading, painful, or persistent rash, signs of infection, or a skin change that concerns you should be discussed with a health professional. Rapid swelling of the mouth or throat or breathing trouble may be an emergency allergic reaction.
