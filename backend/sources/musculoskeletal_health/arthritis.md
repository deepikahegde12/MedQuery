# Arthritis

## What is arthritis?

Arthritis refers to conditions affecting joints and nearby tissues. Common symptoms include joint pain, stiffness, and swelling, but causes and patterns differ. Osteoarthritis involves changes in joint tissues over time; rheumatoid arthritis is an autoimmune disease that can affect joints and other parts of the body.

## Assessment and support

A clinician can assess symptoms, examination findings, and sometimes tests to determine the cause. New severe joint pain, a hot swollen joint with fever, or inability to use a joint should be assessed promptly. Management depends on the specific condition and individual health; this document does not recommend treatment.
