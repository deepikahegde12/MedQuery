# Allergies

## What is an allergy?

An allergy is an immune response to a substance that is usually harmless to most people. Symptoms depend on the person and trigger and may affect the nose, eyes, skin, breathing, or digestion. A health professional can review when symptoms occur and whether testing is appropriate; symptoms alone do not identify a specific allergen.

## Severe reactions

A severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) can be life-threatening. Warning signs can include trouble breathing or swelling of the mouth or throat. Seek emergency help immediately through the local emergency service. People with known severe allergies should follow their clinician-provided emergency plan.
