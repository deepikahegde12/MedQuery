# Kidney health and kidney diseases

## What kidneys do

Kidneys filter blood, remove wastes and extra water, and produce urine. Kidney diseases have many causes, including inherited conditions, injury, infections, stones, and some medicines. Chronic kidney disease can damage kidney function gradually.

## Risk and checks

Diabetes, high blood pressure, and a close family history of kidney disease are among factors associated with increased risk. Kidney problems may not be obvious from symptoms alone. A health professional may use blood and urine tests to assess kidney function. Ask a clinician whether screening is appropriate for an individual's health history; do not change medicines based on this summary.
