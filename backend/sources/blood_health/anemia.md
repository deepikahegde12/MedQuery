# Anemia

## What is anemia?

Anemia is a condition in which the blood does not carry enough oxygen to the body's tissues. There are different types and causes. Possible contributors include blood loss, pregnancy, inherited blood disorders, some chronic conditions, or not getting enough of certain nutrients; the cause cannot be determined from symptoms alone.

## Possible symptoms and assessment

Some people feel tired, cold, dizzy, or irritable, and may have shortness of breath or headaches. A health professional can evaluate symptoms and use blood tests to diagnose anemia and investigate its cause. Treatment depends on the type and cause, so supplements or other treatment should not be started on the basis of this summary alone.
